## P-13
title: IR dust removal stage
milestone: M4
type: feature
size: M
estimate: 1d
legacy: GTP-063
deps: P-10
adr: no
spec: specification section 4.2 step 2; rules/colour-pipeline.md
desc: Remove dust using the IR plane before inversion and plug it into the pipeline.
ac:
- Synthetic dust spots are removed (masked pixels filled within tolerance, others unchanged)
- Threshold and grow are monotonic; no IR plane means the stage is skipped
- The stage is disabled by flag `dust_removal` or `dust.enabled = false`
tech:
FILES: crates/gtp-edit/src/dust.rs; crates/gtp-app/src/pipeline/stages.rs (IR stage before crop).
INTERFACES: `remove_dust(rgb: &mut ImageF32, ir: &ImageF32, d: &Dust) -> DustReport { masked_px }` (normalize IR, threshold, dilate by grow_px, iterative neighbour-weighted fill).
ROUTES / MIGRATIONS: none.
TESTS: synthetic dust, monotonicity, skip without IR, flag off; hardware check on a real IR scan by the human.
DELIVERY (decided when P-13 was approved): the plane is read relative to its own clear level (the 90th percentile of the plane), and a pixel is dust when `ir < clear * (1 - threshold)`, so a higher threshold masks fewer pixels; the mask grows by a square of `grow_px`. The fill closes in from the edge of a spot to its centre (neighbour-weighted, 8-neighbourhood). A plane that masks more than 25 % of the picture, or that is dark everywhere, is ignored, and so is every spot larger than 0.05 % of the picture (found on real LS-50 scans: the film holder shows as a wide dark band in the plane, and filling it smeared streaks over the edge of the picture). Tried on four real scans (Ektacolor, Ektar, two Vision3): 0.02 to 0.09 % of the pixels change and the specks and hairs seen in the picture are gone; the full render of a 22 MP frame with dust removal is about 0.3 s. The report is `DustReport { masked, applied }`. `DevelopService` keeps the infrared plane next to the proxy at the same size (a preview resizes it to the scaled scan); the export reads it at full size. A plane that is missing or does not fit the scan is skipped with a warning, and editing and export go on. The `dust_removal` flag reaches the develop service through `set_dust_removal` (set at start and on every settings change) and the export through a parameter of `export_frames`. The preview shrinks the plane with the scan, so fine specks lose contrast there; the export is exact. Real LS-50 scans are for the human to check.

## P-29
title: Wavelet noise reduction that keeps grain texture
milestone: M6
type: feature
size: M
estimate: 2d
deps: P-27
adr: yes
spec: specification sections 4 and 7
desc: P-27 reduces noise with a guided filter, which smooths flat areas well but treats fine grain and coarse grain alike, so a strong setting can leave a picture looking plastic. Wavelet shrinkage splits the picture into scales of detail and reduces each by its own amount, so coarse blotches can go while fine grain texture stays. The owner asked on 2026-11-11 for this as a later step. Decide by measurement whether it replaces the guided filter or sits beside it, and add it.
ac:
- An ADR compares wavelet shrinkage with the guided filter of P-27 on real scans of at least three film stocks (fine grain, coarse grain, pushed): what each keeps and removes, and the cost on a 24 megapixel frame; it decides whether the wavelet method replaces the guided filter or is offered beside it
- The luminance noise reduction keeps fine grain texture at settings where coarse noise is clearly reduced, shown by a measure the ADR defines (for example the energy left in the finest scale against that in the coarser ones)
- The existing sliders keep their meaning: a recipe saved with P-27 loads and renders as before, or the ADR states the change and the specification and the pull request say it plainly
- Any new control (for example how much fine grain to keep) is a recipe field with a default that changes nothing, approved by the owner with the plan
- With the amounts at 0 the stage does nothing and the render is bit for bit the same as without it
- The export of a 24 megapixel frame stays within the export budget of the specification; the measured time is in the pull request
- Tests: identity at 0, a flat noisy patch, a step edge, grain kept at the finest scale, preview against full size, old recipes loading, a timing test marked ignored; component tests for any new control; bindings regenerated if the recipe changes; axe clean
tech:
FILES: crates/gtp-edit (the wavelet transform and the shrinkage), crates/gtp-app/src/pipeline, crates/gtp-core/src/recipe only if a control is added, the Detail panel, en.json, rules/colour-pipeline.md, decisions.
ROUTES: none new; the Recipe gains a field only if the plan asks for one and the owner approves it.
MIGRATIONS: none.
TESTS: as in ac.
NOTES: the method named in the ADR of P-27 as possible later. A usual form is the undecimated (a trous) transform with a B3 spline kernel and a soft threshold per scale, in the same working space as P-27. Written in Rust in gtp-edit; a new dependency needs the approval of the owner. No learned or AI model: that is a non-goal. Out of scope: grain synthesis, reduction by mask, removing dust or scratches.
