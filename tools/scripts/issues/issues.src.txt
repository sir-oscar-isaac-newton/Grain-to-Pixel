## P-13
title: IR dust removal stage
milestone: M4
type: feature
size: M
estimate: 1d
legacy: GTP-063
deps: P-10
adr: no
spec: specification section 4.2 step 2; rules/colour-pipeline.md
desc: Remove dust using the IR plane before inversion and plug it into the pipeline.
ac:
- Synthetic dust spots are removed (masked pixels filled within tolerance, others unchanged)
- Threshold and grow are monotonic; no IR plane means the stage is skipped
- The stage is disabled by flag `dust_removal` or `dust.enabled = false`
tech:
FILES: crates/gtp-edit/src/dust.rs; crates/gtp-app/src/pipeline/stages.rs (IR stage before crop).
INTERFACES: `remove_dust(rgb: &mut ImageF32, ir: &ImageF32, d: &Dust) -> DustReport { masked_px }` (normalize IR, threshold, dilate by grow_px, iterative neighbour-weighted fill).
ROUTES / MIGRATIONS: none.
TESTS: synthetic dust, monotonicity, skip without IR, flag off; hardware check on a real IR scan by the human.
DELIVERY (decided when P-13 was approved): the plane is read relative to its own clear level (the 90th percentile of the plane), and a pixel is dust when `ir < clear * (1 - threshold)`, so a higher threshold masks fewer pixels; the mask grows by a square of `grow_px`. The fill closes in from the edge of a spot to its centre (neighbour-weighted, 8-neighbourhood). A plane that masks more than 25 % of the picture, or that is dark everywhere, is ignored, and so is every spot larger than 0.05 % of the picture (found on real LS-50 scans: the film holder shows as a wide dark band in the plane, and filling it smeared streaks over the edge of the picture). Tried on four real scans (Ektacolor, Ektar, two Vision3): 0.02 to 0.09 % of the pixels change and the specks and hairs seen in the picture are gone; the full render of a 22 MP frame with dust removal is about 0.3 s. The report is `DustReport { masked, applied }`. `DevelopService` keeps the infrared plane next to the proxy at the same size (a preview resizes it to the scaled scan); the export reads it at full size. A plane that is missing or does not fit the scan is skipped with a warning, and editing and export go on. The `dust_removal` flag reaches the develop service through `set_dust_removal` (set at start and on every settings change) and the export through a parameter of `export_frames`. The preview shrinks the plane with the scan, so fine specks lose contrast there; the export is exact. Real LS-50 scans are for the human to check.
